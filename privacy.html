ASK A NEIGHBOUR

Privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026

Ask a Neighbour helps Leaside neighbours find trades and household help they trust. We collect only what the app needs to do that, we never sell your information, and there are no ads. This policy explains what we keep, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect and why

  • Your account: your email address (to send sign-in codes), first name, the street area you choose (like "Bayview & Millwood"), and whether your profile is public or private.
  • Checking your address: your postal code and a photo of a recent utility bill. Only our admin team sees the bill, and we delete it as soon as we approve or decline. We keep your postal code and an approximate map point for it, privately, to confirm you live in the area.
  • What you add: your reviews (rating, tags and any text), your Go-To Pros and Want to Try lists, who you follow, your seasonal checklist ticks, and the invite link you share.
  • Household helpers: your photo, first name, bio, hourly rate, availability, experience and roles, plus your home postal code (private, used only to check distance). Your photo ID, police check and certificates are seen only by our admin team and deleted once we decide. We keep only that each one was verified, and when. We contact your references using the details you give us, and for babysitters aged 16 or 17, a parent or guardian by email.
  • Messages and reports: messages between residents and helpers, and any concern you report, so we can keep people safe.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on: a code that lets us send notifications to your phone, and which kinds you want. Notifications never include message text.
  • Usage analytics, if you leave it on: which screens and features are used, your device type and app version. It is linked only to a random account number, never your name, email, address or anything you write. You can switch it off any time in Profile.

Who can see what

  • Everyone using the app: reviews, with your first name and street area if your profile is public. With a private profile, your reviews still count toward a company's Neighbour Score but show no name.
  • Neighbours you allow: if your profile is public, neighbours can find you, follow you, and see your reviews, badges and leaderboard place.
  • People you share a list link with: the list's name and the companies and helpers in it. Nothing about you.
  • Our admin team: what's needed to check addresses, approve helpers and handle reports.
  • Nobody else. We don't sell or rent your information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage. Your information may be stored on servers outside Canada.
  • Google Maps provides company names, ratings, reviews and logos. We send Google your search, not who you are, and we don't store Google's content. Company logos load directly from Google, so Google may see your device's internet address. Google's privacy policy applies to what Google provides.
  • Apple Maps draws the map of your saved companies.
  • Expo delivers our push notifications to Apple. It receives your phone's notification code and the notification's text, nothing else.
  • PostHog provides our usage analytics, if you leave it on.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Utility bills and helper documents are deleted as soon as we make a decision.
  • Everything else stays while you have an account. You can delete your account any time in Profile, under Delete account. That permanently removes your login, profile, reviews, lists, follows, messages, invites, checklist and any helper profile and files. Our hosting provider's backups may keep a copy for a short time before it expires.
  • Signing out or deleting your account also clears what the app saved on your phone.

Your choices and rights

  • Make your profile private, switch off analytics, or delete your account, all from Profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct it, by email.
  • If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Age

  • You must be 16 or older to use Ask a Neighbour. Babysitters aged 16 or 17 can join only with a parent or guardian's consent.

Changes

  • If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app if the change is important.
